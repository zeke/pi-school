  Rookie (never written code), Dabbler (tinkered a bit), Builder (builds

Pi will ask you a few optional questions about your background and preferences. Your answers help tailor the rest of the course — adjusting detail level, choosing relevant examples, and matching your comfort with the terminal.

The first question is about pace: fast track (blaze through, no quizzes, short answers) or thorough (quizzes, fuller explanations). Fast track is the default, so if you skip this question — or the whole interview — that's what you get.

All questions are optional, and you can change any answer later just by asking Pi to update it.
